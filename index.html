What it does

One plugin. Three analysis modes.
Every metering tool you need.

◈

M/S · L/R · Tonal/Transient

Switch between Mid/Side, Left/Right, and Transient/Tone channel modes. All three visualized simultaneously with color-coded curves.

〜

Real-time Spectrum Analyzer

FFT 2048–16384 points. Fractional-octave smoothing (Off / 1/3 / 1/6 / 1/12), spectrum tilt 0 to +6 dB/oct, draggable curve ordering, Fill modes (None / Gradient / Solid), and 256 log-spaced path points at 60 fps.

∿

Oscilloscope View

Time-domain waveform view with variable time window (100 ms – 4 s) and vertical scale. Spectral coloring highlights harmonics. Optional CRT filter with denser grid graticule for a warmer, analog-feeling trace.

◎

Goniometer & Correlation

Lissajous stereo field display with phosphor persistence, L/R phase correlation bar (−1 to +1), and per-octave stereo width readout.

▬

LUFS / Peak Metering

Integrated loudness and peak metering across both primary and secondary channels. True-peak indication, selectable weighting.

❄

Ghost, Freeze & Peak Hold

Reference spectrum overlay, freeze the display for detailed inspection, and infinite peak hold — all independently toggled.

⊞

Waterfall View

A scrolling reassigned spectrogram snaps energy to its true time–frequency location — pure tones paint razor-sharp lines instead of smeared bands. FFT 2k / 4k / 8k, freeze, and reassignment on/off toggle.

⊡

Band Audition (Solo / Cut)

Click any of seven frequency-band tabs to solo it and hear it in isolation. Cmd-click to latch; right-click to cut (notch) instead. Solo = blue/solid, cut = red/dashed — colour-blind friendly.

⊟

Split View (Up / Down)

Stack Primary and Secondary into top and bottom halves for both the spectrum and oscilloscope — compare channels without overlap, side-by-side in a single view.

Talking points

Angles that resonate with audio audiences.

01
The Waterfall view is the screenshot-grab moment. A reassigned spectrogram is rare in free tools — energy snaps to its exact time/frequency, so tones draw as razor-sharp lines. It's the kind of visual that makes great B-roll and instantly communicates that this isn't a toy.
02
The "free but professional" angle. gFractor isn't a stripped-down freemium tool — it's a fully-featured analyzer with no paid tier. The argument: serious metering used to require paid tools like SPAN Plus, iZotope Insight, or Voxengo SPAN. gFractor matches or exceeds them for free.
03
M/S is the underrated superpower. Most producers only use L/R analysis. Mid/Side reveals what the stereo field is actually doing — is the mid energy balanced with the sides? Is the bass mono? M/S mode is the lens that answers those questions.
04
Four modes = four different views of your mix. Mid/Side for stereo field, Left/Right for channel imbalance, Transient/Tone to see attack vs. sustain energy, and mono-sum L+R for quick mono-compatibility checks. Each mode is a different diagnostic lens — together they replace a full metering rack.
05
The goniometer alone is worth the install. Most producers have never used a goniometer. It visualizes the stereo Lissajous figure in real time — a narrow vertical line means mono-compatible, a wide figure-8 means wide stereo. Invaluable for mastering.
06
No registration. No telemetry. No strings. Growl Audio is explicit: no account, no analytics, no data collection. In an era where "free" usually means "we take something else", that's a genuine differentiator worth calling out.
07
Version 1.2.0 is out now — cover it fresh. gFractor 1.2.0 just shipped stable. Your audience gets the full feature set — Waterfall view, band audition, split view, four channel modes — from day one.
