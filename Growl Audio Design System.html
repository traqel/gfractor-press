14 / 15
The discipline

Do's & don'ts.

Do
→ Keep teal under ~10% — active, primary, or measurable only.
→ Machine every edge to 0px. No exceptions; the hard edge is the signature.
→ Build hierarchy from weight & tracking — there is one typeface.
→ Keep surfaces flat at rest; let shadow appear only on state.
→ Tint neutrals toward 195° in OKLCH; clear WCAG AA on every token.
→ Show real product imagery. Proof over promises.
Don't
✕ Let it read as a generic dark-mode SaaS template.
✕ Drift toward a cluttered legacy plugin wall.
✕ Adopt deal-marketplace urgency — timers, "90% OFF".
✕ Use pure #fff or #000 for type.
✕ Put a >1px colored side-stripe on anything but a feature tag.
✕ Add a second font, gradient text, decorative glass, or bounce easing.
